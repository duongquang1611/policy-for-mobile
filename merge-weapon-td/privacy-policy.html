Privacy Policy

Merge Weapon Tower Defense
Last updated: 2026-08-24

Merge Weapon Tower Defense ("the app") is a free mobile game. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data we store on your device

Game progress (level, coins, gems, skill upgrades, settings) is saved only on your device in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded by us. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising ID and coarse device information to serve and measure ads. In the EEA/UK you are asked for consent through Google's UMP consent form and can change your choice anytime in Settings → Privacy options. See Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Watching rewarded ads (weapon chests, ×2 fire rate, offline income ×2, victory rewards ×2, revive, free coins and gems) grants in-game rewards. Rewarded ads only play when you choose to watch them.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment details.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are requested with child-directed treatment where required by law.

Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation in your device's Google settings.
  • Change your EEA/UK ad-consent choice anytime in Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app.
