Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026 · com.stadiumrush.gemini
Quang Studio · duongquang16111997@gmail.com

The short version. Stadium Rush has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress never leaves your phone. The only third parties involved are Google AdMob, which serves the ads, and Google Play Billing, which handles purchases.

What the game stores on your device

Progress — which zones you have unlocked, your upgrade levels, coins, gems, the skins you own, and your daily-reward, mission and Match Day state — is written to a JSON file inside the app's own private storage. Settings (music, sound effects, haptics, language) are kept in Unity's PlayerPrefs.

None of this is sent anywhere, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising

Stadium Rush shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect a device advertising identifier, coarse device and network information, and ad-interaction data. See Google's Privacy & Terms and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app shows Google's UMP consent form on first launch. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options.

Buying Remove ads or Weekly VIP turns banners and interstitials off.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Restoring purchases asks the store what you already own.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ad requests are made with the standard child-directed treatment flags Google's SDK provides where required.

Your rights

Because nothing is sent to us, there is no account to export or delete. To remove every trace of the game, uninstall it. For anything else, email duongquang16111997@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published at this same URL — the one the app links to from Settings.
