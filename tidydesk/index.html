Tidy Desk ASMR

A S M R

A calm organising game about the most satisfying thing there is: putting everything back where it belongs. Drag each object into its faint silhouette, feel it click, then pull the slider and watch BEFORE turn into AFTER.

Six hand-made desks from school and the office — a student desk, a teacher's desk, an art corner, an office desk, a locker and a late-night ASMR desk. Each has its own little ritual: staple the papers, pour the coffee, scrub the paint stain, coil the cable, sweep the shelf, light the candle.

What it collects

Nothing. Progress and settings stay on your device; there is no account and no server. Ads are served by Google AdMob — the details are in the privacy policy.
