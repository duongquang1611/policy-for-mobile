Tidy Desk ASMR (com.tidydesk.gemini), published by Quang Studio, is a single-player game with no account system and no server of its own. This policy says exactly what the app does with data.

What stays on your device

Your progress — which desks you have tidied and their stars, coins, gems, hints, decor you own, daily-reward and mission state — is written to a JSON file in the app's own private storage. Settings (sound, music, haptics, notifications, language) are kept in Unity's PlayerPrefs. None of this leaves your device, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

We collect nothing. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter and no login. We never see your progress, your device or your email unless you write to us yourself.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure them, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect a device advertising identifier, an IP address, coarse device and network information, and ad-interaction data. See Google's Privacy & Terms and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app shows Google's UMP consent form on first launch. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the game.

Buying Remove ads or Weekly VIP turns banners and interstitials off. Rewarded ads are always optional — nothing in the game is gated behind watching one.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. "Restore" asks the store what you already own.

Permissions

  • Network — used only to load ads and to talk to Google Play Billing.
  • Vibration — used only for the haptic tick when a piece snaps into place. Turn it off in Settings.

The game asks for no camera, no location, no contacts and no storage permission.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ad requests are made with the child-directed treatment flags Google's SDK provides where they are required.

Your rights

Because nothing is sent to us, there is no account to export or delete. To remove every trace of the game, uninstall it or use Android's Clear storage. For anything else, email us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the new version is published at this same URL — the one the app links to from Settings.
